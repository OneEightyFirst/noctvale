Noctvale Quick Reference

Round at a Glance

Start of round: Count each player's Active and Downed fighters — that is their activation count. Do not count Stunned, Out of Action, or Escaped fighters. The player with the lower activation count has initiative and gains Overwatch tokens equal to the difference between the two counts. Do not recalculate activation count, initiative, or tokens during the round.

Activations: Starting with the player who has initiative, players alternate activating one fighter at a time. Each fighter performs 2 actions in any order.

End of round: The round ends when every fighter who can activate has activated. Remove unspent Overwatch tokens. Begin a new round.

Overwatch

  • Start of round: Lower activation count player receives tokens equal to the difference between the two counts.
  • Overwatch action: During a fighter's activation, spend 1 action and 1 Overwatch token to go on Overwatch. That fighter may later perform any 1 action as a reaction when an enemy completes an action. Their remaining action may be used before or after declaring Overwatch.
  • Reaction timing: A fighter on Overwatch may react whenever an enemy completes an action during the round — even after you have run out of fighters to activate.
  • End of round: Remove unspent tokens.

Morale

  • Lost: Out of Action or Stunned. Active and Downed are not lost.
  • Routing: More than half of the retinue's starting fighters are lost (e.g. 5 deployed → routing at 3 lost).
  • Test: At the start of each activation while routing, before actions — Will check. Summon auto-pass if any friendly within 6" and line of sight. Modifiers: +1 per friendly within 6"; +2 for Leader within 6"; +1 if Fearless.
  • Fail: Spend both actions fleeing toward nearest battlefield edge.

Engagement

  • Engagement range is 1". No facing.
  • Gang Up: Friendly also engaged with same enemy → +1 die to Strike Pool.
  • Multiple enemies: Split Strike Pool among standing enemies; prioritize standing over Downed/Stunned.

Noctvale Quick Reference

Attack Sequence

Step 1 — Build the Strike Pool: Fighter Might + Skill + weapon +Might / +Skill, to a maximum of 15 dice. Firearms and damage spells use a flat Strike Pool instead.

Step 2 — Roll to hit: Melee → Close Combat. Ranged → Ranged Combat. Apply Hit attribute modifiers.

Step 3 — Determine criticals: Weapon triangle, magic triangle, Aim, or firearms. Critical hits cannot be blocked.

Step 4 — Roll defense: Build the pool: defender Might + Skill, modified by armor before rolling (Light +1 Sk, Medium +1 Mt, Heavy +2 Mt −1 Sk), plus bonuses. Roll all as Df checks. Attacker wins ties by color; a shield shifts ties to the defender.

Step 5 — Apply Wounds (below).

Apply Wounds

Each unblocked hit inflicts 1 Wound.

  • 0 Wounds → Downed
  • Unblocked hit on Downed → Stunned
  • Unblocked hit on Stunned → Out of Action

Downed — full defense pool. Stunned — Might dice only.

Defense

  • Dual wielding: two one-handed melee weapons add both weapon profiles to the Strike Pool, max 15 dice. Use only the primary weapon's type and special rules. No shield.
  • Might blocks Might hits; Skill blocks Skill hits.
  • 2 Might may block 1 Skill hit; 2 Skill may block 1 Might hit.
  • Cover: +1 Skill defense die.

Armor & Shields

Armor adds dice to the pool before rolling. Shields change who wins ties. Effects stack. Ties: when blocks = hits in a color category, attacker wins — 1 hit gets through.

Critical Hits

A natural 6 in the Strike Pool is a critical hit only when the attacker has triangle advantage over the defender — weapon triangle in melee, magic triangle on spell attacks. Firearms crit against all targets. Aim adds crit on 6 to non-firearm ranged weapons, or improves an existing crit threshold to 5+. Crits cannot be blocked. All other 6s are normal hits.

Ranged

  • LoS required; friendlies do not block; enemies do.
  • Cover: terrain > 1" from attacker → +1 Sk defense die.
  • Clustered enemies within 1" → Skill check.
  • Friendly fire when target engaged with friendlies → Ranged Combat check.
  • Line of fire (firearms/spells): miss → 1d6, on 1 hit intervening friendly.
  • Ranged Reaction: 1 free action after being targeted by Ranged (not Cast).
