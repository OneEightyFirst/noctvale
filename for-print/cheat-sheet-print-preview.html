Noctvale Quick Reference

Round at a Glance

Start of round: Count each player's Active and Downed fighters — that is their activation count. Do not count Stunned, Out of Action, or Escaped fighters. Round 1 uses battle-set-up initiative. Later rounds: lower count has initiative; on a tie, initiative switches. The lower-count player gains Pass tokens equal to the difference. Do not recalculate during the round.

Activations: Starting with the player who has initiative, players alternate activating one fighter at a time. Each fighter performs 2 actions in any order. A player may spend a Pass token instead of activating a fighter, but cannot pass on consecutive turns to activate. A Stunned fighter restored before its player finishes activating may act later this round: 1 action if Active, or Recover only if Downed.

End of round: If one player runs out of fighters first, the other activates all remaining fighters normally. The round ends when every eligible fighter has activated. Remove unspent Pass tokens.

Pass Tokens

  • Start of round: Lower activation count player receives tokens equal to the difference between the two counts.
  • Pass: When it is your turn to activate a fighter, spend 1 Pass token instead. No fighter activates, and the opposing player takes the next activation.
  • No consecutive passes: After passing, you must activate a fighter on your next turn to activate before you can spend another token.
  • End of round: Remove unspent tokens.

Morale

  • Lost: Out of Action or Stunned. Active and Downed are not lost.
  • Routing: More than half of the retinue's starting fighters are lost (e.g. 5 deployed → routing at 3 lost).
  • Test: At the start of each activation while routing, before actions — Will check. Summon auto-pass if any friendly within 6" and line of sight. Modifiers: +1 per other friendly within 6"; a Leader gives +2 instead of +1; +1 if Fearless.
  • Fail: Spend both actions fleeing toward nearest battlefield edge.

Engagement

  • Engagement range is 1". No facing.
  • Gang Up: Friendly also engaged with same enemy → +1 die to Strike Pool.
  • Multiple enemies: Split Strike Pool among standing enemies; prioritize standing over Downed/Stunned.

Attack Sequence

Step 1Build the Strike Pool from the attack profile, max 15 dice. A value with a + adds to the fighter's matching attribute. Some weapons and spells list a fixed Might or Skill value without a +; use exactly that many dice instead of adding the fighter's matching attribute. Dual wielding: add both weapons' +Mt and +Sk, max 15 dice; primary weapon's type and rules apply; no shield.

Step 2Roll to hit: Melee → Close Combat. Ranged → Ranged Combat. Apply Hit attribute modifiers. Natural 1 always misses; natural 6 always hits.

Step 3Determine criticals: Weapon triangle, magic triangle, Aim, or firearms. Critical hits cannot be blocked.

Step 4Roll defense: defender Might + Skill, modified by armor before rolling (Light +1 Sk, Medium +1 Mt, Heavy +2 Mt −1 Sk), plus bonuses. Roll all as Defense checks.

Step 5Apply Wounds (below).

Apply Wounds

Resolve all unblocked hits from one attack together. Each inflicts 1 Wound.

  • Active reduced to exactly 0 Wounds → Downed
  • Active reduced below 0 Wounds → set to 0 Wounds and become Stunned
  • Unblocked hit on Downed → Stunned
  • Unblocked hit on Stunned → Out of Action

Downed — full defense pool. Stunned — Might dice only.

Defense

  • Might blocks Might hits; Skill blocks Skill hits.
  • Dodge: Active only; vs Melee/Ranged, spend 2 Skill successes to block 1 Might hit. If still Active after Wounds, move up to 2" directly away from attacker.
  • Shove: Active only; vs engaged Melee attacker, spend 2 Might successes to block 1 Skill hit. If still Active after Wounds, push attacker up to 2" directly away; stop at contact. Skill check to avoid a ledge.
  • Maneuver movement: not an action/reaction/Move/Retreat/Charge; does not trigger Ranged Reaction, Companion co-movement, or failed-Retreat attacks. Cannot pass through bases.
  • Downed/Stunned: cannot use defensive maneuvers.
  • Cover: +1 Skill defense die; +2 if target is within 1" of the intervening terrain.
  • Ties: equal blocks and hits — attacker wins, 1 hit gets through.

Armor & Shields

Armor modifies the pool before rolling. Shields modify the Df threshold on specific dice. Effects stack.

Critical Hits

Natural 6 = crit when attacker has triangle advantage — weapon triangle in melee, magic triangle on spells. Firearms crit vs all. Aim adds crit on 6 to non-firearm ranged, or improves threshold to 5+. All other 6s are normal hits.

Ranged

  • LoS required; friendlies do not block; enemies do. Minimum range is 3" unless noted.
  • Cover: intervening terrain > 1" from attacker → +1 Sk defense die; target within 1" of that terrain → +2 Sk dice instead.
  • Clustered enemies within 1" → Skill check; on a fail, redirect to the other nearby enemy (randomly select if multiple).
  • Friendly fire: if a friendly is in the line of fire or engaged with the target, natural 1s in the Strike Pool hit the nearest at-risk friendly instead.
  • Ranged Reaction: after a Ranged Attack Action fully resolves, an Active target may take 1 free action. Not during its player's activations; not after Cast; cannot trigger another Ranged Reaction.
