# What You Need to Play


To play Noctvale, each player needs:

- Miniatures or tokens on bases.
- A **3' × 3'** battlefield with terrain.
- At least **10 d6**: **5** in one color and **5** in another.
- A measuring tool marked in **"**.
- Counters or markers for **Hidden**, **Downed**, **Stunned**, **Poisoned**, **Weakened**, **Enfeebled**, **Bleeding**, **Blinded**, **Fear**, **Panic**, **Insanity**, body markers, and **Overwatch tokens**.
- Template markers: a **standard flame template**, a **3" blast template**, a **5" blast template**, and a **scatter die**. You can print the template markers provided at the back of this book.

Use the scenario rules if a battle gives different setup, terrain, deployment, objective, or reward instructions.
