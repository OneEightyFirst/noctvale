## How a Battle Plays Out

A **battle** is fought over a series of **rounds**. In each round, every fighter who can still fight gets a chance to act, unless their player chooses to hold back. When every eligible fighter has activated or been deliberately skipped, the round ends and a new one begins.

Noctvale does not use clock phases or simultaneous movement. Players alternate activations — one fighter at a time — for the whole round. A fighter who moves now may be **Downed** before they act again. A fighter who holds back may yet ruin an enemy's plan from the shadows.

> **The turn at a glance**
>
> **1. Start of round** — Count **activation count**. Determine initiative. Issue **Overwatch tokens**.
>
> **2. Alternating activations** — Starting with the player who has initiative, alternate activating one fighter at a time. Each activation is **2 actions**. **Stunned** fighters cannot be chosen.
>
> **3. End of round** — When every fighter who can activate has activated or **skipped their activation**, begin a new round.

---

## Activation Count

Before anything else each round, count how many fighters each player can still bring to bear. This number is that player's **activation count**.

Count every **Active** and **Downed** fighter on the board. Do not count fighters who are **Stunned**, **Out of Action**, or **Escaped**.

Activation count sets initiative and **Overwatch tokens** at the start of the round. Do not recalculate either during the round — even if fighters fall, recover, or flee mid-round.

---

## Initiative

Initiative decides who activates first. It can shift as casualties mount.

**First round:** Use the initiative determined during [battle set-up](core-rules.md#battle-setup).

**Later rounds:** The player with the **lower activation count** has initiative and activates first. If both players have the same activation count, the player who had initiative last round keeps it.

---

## Overwatch Tokens

Once initiative is set, compare the two activation counts. The player with the **lower** count receives **Overwatch tokens** equal to the difference.

Overwatch tokens represent held breath and ready steel — the outnumbered watching for the perfect moment to strike. A player may spend a token to react when an enemy completes an action, or may **skip a fighter's activation** to give that fighter a token of their own.

*(Full rules for spending tokens: [Overwatch](core-rules.md#overwatch).)*

---

## Activating Fighters

Starting with the player who has initiative, players take turns activating **one fighter at a time**.

When a fighter activates, they may perform **2 actions** chosen from the action list — **Move**, **Melee**, **Ranged**, **Cast**, and the rest. Actions may be taken in any combination and in any order.

Only fighters who can activate may be chosen:

- **Stunned**, **Out of Action**, and **Escaped** fighters cannot be chosen.
- If a **Stunned** fighter recovers later in the round, they may activate before the round ends; see [Stunned](core-rules.md#stunned).

After one fighter's activation is complete, the other player activates one of their fighters. This continues until the round ends.

---

## Ending a Round

The round ends when every fighter who can activate has activated or **skipped their activation**.

If one player runs out of eligible fighters before the other, the remaining player **activates their remaining fighters normally**, one at a time. The player who ran out may spend any unspent **Overwatch tokens** as reactions during these activations.

Begin a new round at **Start of round**.

## Example: A Round in the Ruins

*Two retinues clash in the shell of a Valecorian tollhouse. The Phoenix Guard player has four fighters still standing; the Grave Wardens player has six.*

**Start of round.** The Phoenix Guard counts four activations; the Grave Wardens count six. The Guard has initiative — fewer fighters, fewer activations — and receives **2 Overwatch tokens**.

The Guard activates their crossbowman first. He **Aims**, then fires at a necromancer (**Ranged**). The necromancer survives and **Moves** behind a collapsed wall — a **Ranged Reaction** after the shot resolves.

The Grave Wardens activate their captain, who **Charges** the Guard's spearman and **Melee**s. The spearman takes a wound but holds. On the Guard's next activation, the spearman **Retreats** — and fails the test. The captain's free **Melee** leaves the spearman **Downed**.

The round continues, activation by activation, until every eligible fighter on both sides has acted or been skipped. Only then does the second round begin — and the counts, initiative, and Overwatch tokens are set fresh.
