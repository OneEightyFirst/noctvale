## Design Notes

- Humans are the baseline: average across all stats, 3 Wounds
- Elves are faster with better Luck but are fragile (1 Wound)
- Dwarves are slow with worse Luck but are durable (4 Wounds)
- All three species share identical CC, RC, St, T, Sa, and Df at baseline — differentiation comes from M, L, and W
