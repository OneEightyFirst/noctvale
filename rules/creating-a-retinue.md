# Creating a Retinue

Your **retinue** is the warband you field in skirmish battles and campaigns — a roster of fighters, their equipment, and (if your Domain allows) their spells. This guide walks you through building a legal roster from scratch.

Every retinue is built in two layers:

1. **Identity** — Archetype, Domain, and Tradition. This sets how your retinue fights and what powers it can wield.
2. **Roster** — spend **Crowns** to recruit fighters, buy gear, and apply poisons.

---

## Your Budget


| Game type               | Starting Crowns | Retinue Rating                              |
| ----------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Skirmish / new campaign | **1000**        | **1000** (Veteran Value 0 on every fighter) |


- Every Crown cost in this guide is a multiple of **5 Crowns**.
- At a typical roster size (7–10 fighters), plan to spend roughly **450–550 Crowns** on fighters and **450–550 Crowns** on weapons, armor, and consumables.
- **Named factions** (Phoenix Guard, Silver Hunt, Village Watch, etc.) are optional flavor presets — the same rules apply whether you use one or build your own combination.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Archetype, Domain, and Tradition

### Archetype

Your Archetype sets roster size, which **weapon proficiencies** each fighter may
take, armor access, shield access, firearm tier access, and how many casters
you may field.


| Archetype   | Proficiency menu                          | Armor        | Shields                       | Firearms tier (Mortal only)          | Max casters |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| **Knights** | One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Thrown   | Up to Heavy  | Buckler, Shield, Tower Shield | Basic (Musket, Blunderbuss)          | 1           |
| **Hunters** | One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Thrown   | Up to Medium | Buckler, Shield               | Basic + Refined (Pistol, Long Rifle) | 1           |
| **Folk**    | One-Handed, Archery, Thrown               | Up to Light  | Buckler, Shield               | Basic (Musket, Blunderbuss)          | 1           |
| **Cult**    | One-Handed, Archery                       | None         | None                          | None                                 | 3           |


Each fighter must have the matching proficiency to equip a weapon. Assign
**Proficiency** at roster creation (see [Feats](feats.md#feat-picks-at-roster-creation)).

**Firearms** (firearms and bombs) require the **Firearms** proficiency from the
**Mortal** Domain feat list. Magic and gunpowder do not mix — a retinue has one
path or the other.

**Cult** cannot choose the Mortal Domain.

### Domain

Your Domain sets your spell list (if any) and whether you may use gunpowder. **Mortal** is the only Domain without magic.

### Tradition

After Archetype and Domain, choose **one Tradition** for the whole retinue. Traditions grant a single retinue-wide special rule. Pick a Tradition allowed for your Archetype on your chosen Domain:


| Domain         | Knights                      | Hunters                  | Folk                     | Cult                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| **Light**      | Crusaders, Paladins          | Paladins, Luminaries     | Crusaders, Penitents     | Penitents, Luminaries      |
| **Arcane**     | Spellblades, Sorcerers       | Spellblades, Runecasters | Ritualists, Runecasters  | Ritualists, Sorcerers      |
| **Infernal**   | Hellknights, Damned          | Hellknights, Binders     | Diabolists, Damned       | Diabolists, Binders        |
| **Nature**     | Grove-keepers, Hedge-walkers | Beastmen, Hedge-walkers  | Witches, Grove-keepers   | Witches, Beastmen          |
| **Necromancy** | Sepulchers, Wightlords       | Bone-priests, Wightlords | Bell-keepers, Sepulchers | Bell-keepers, Bone-priests |
| **Blood**      | Vampires, Dynasts            | Dynasts, Revelers        | Revelers, Courtiers      | Vampires, Courtiers        |
| **Mortal**     | Constables, Ironbound        | Zealots, Alchemists      | Constables, Alchemists   | —                          |


Choose one Tradition from the matrix above, then record its special rule below. Some Traditions change fighter or equipment costs.

### Tradition special rules

#### Light

**Crusaders:** Friendly fighters within **1"** of at least one other friendly fighter add **+1 Sa** to Fear, Panic, and Insanity tests.

**Paladins:** Once per round, when a friendly fighter becomes **Downed**, one friendly fighter within **6"** may move up to **3"** toward them. This move cannot enter engagement range.

**Penitents:** While a friendly fighter has fewer than their starting Wounds, they gain **+1" M**.

**Luminaries:** At the start of each round, choose one enemy fighter within line of sight of any friendly fighter. That enemy loses **Hidden**.

#### Arcane

**Spellblades:** Fighters in this retinue cost **+5 Crowns**. Melee weapons they carry gain **Arcane** — when attacking with that weapon, change 1 failed Strike Pool die into a normal hit (keeps color; cannot become a critical).

**Ritualists:** Before deployment, place one ritual circle wholly within your deployment zone. Friendly **Caster** fighters within **1"** of it add **+1** to casting rolls. If they roll a Mishap, they suffer **1 Wound** after resolving it.

**Sorcerers:** Friendly **Caster** fighters may each know 1 additional spell for **+10 Crowns**. When a Caster who knows an additional spell this way rolls a Mishap, they suffer **1 Wound** after resolving it.

**Runecasters:** This retinue may buy Rune-stones for **15 Crowns**. Rune-stones take 1 weapon slot. Once per battle, after a roll to hit, defense, stat roll, or casting roll, discard the Rune-stones to reroll that roll (second result stands). Remove after the battle.

#### Infernal

**Diabolists:** Once per battle, at the start of a friendly fighter's activation, that fighter may suffer **1 Wound** to gain **+1 Mt** and **+1 Sa** until end of battle. If they become **Downed**, they lose this bonus and suffer **−1 Mt** and **−1 Sa** until end of battle (Sa cannot drop below 1).

**Binders:** This retinue may recruit Daemons as Rank fighters. Daemon profiles and recruitment costs are not yet published — consult your group before using this Tradition.

**Hellknights:** Armor bought by this retinue costs **+10 Crowns**. Friendly fighters wearing armor gain **Fear**.

**Damned:** Non-Caster fighters in this retinue cost **−10 Crowns**. When rolling on the Casualty Table for one of those fighters, subtract **1** from the roll.

#### Nature

**Witches:** Once per round, when an enemy fighter within **12"** of a friendly **Caster** makes a stat roll or casting roll, subtract **1** from that roll.

**Grove-keepers:** After terrain is set, choose one terrain piece outside both deployment zones. Friendly fighters within **1"** of it add **+1** to **Recover** rolls.

**Beastmen:** Choose one beast-mark: **Wolf**, **Rat**, **Bear**, or **Serpent**. All fighters cost **+10 Crowns** and gain the chosen rule:

- **Wolf:** Fighting Claws (0-slot melee: +2 Mt, +1 Sk, Axe type)
- **Rat:** May carry 1 additional one-handed weapon (not shield, firearm, bomb, or two-handed)
- **Bear:** +1 Mt, −1 Sa (Sa cannot drop below 1)
- **Serpent:** When attacking with melee, 1 unblocked hit may inflict **Poisoned** instead of 1 Wound

**Hedge-walkers:** During deployment, up to 2 friendly fighters may start **Hidden** if each is within **1"** of terrain and outside the enemy deployment zone.

#### Necromancy

**Bell-keepers:** Once per battle, when a friendly fighter is taken **Out of Action**, replace them with a Zombie until end of battle. After the battle, roll on the Casualty Table for the original fighter. If Slain and the Zombie is not Out of Action, the Zombie joins your roster.

**Bone-priests:** This retinue may recruit Skeletons as Rank fighters up to its normal Rank cap (4 for Hunters, 7 for Cult). Skeletons count toward max fighters, cannot gain XP, buy equipment, carry scenario rewards, or use post-battle advancement. See [Profiles](profiles.md) for the Skeleton profile.

**Sepulchers:** When a friendly fighter becomes **Downed** or **Stunned**, they gain **Fear** until they become Active or Out of Action.

**Wightlords:** All fighters cost **+20 Crowns** and gain **Undead** and **Fearless**.

#### Blood

**Vampires:** All fighters cost **+20 Crowns**. When a friendly fighter takes an enemy Out of Action with **Melee** or **Mercy Kill**, restore **1 Wound** to that friendly fighter.

**Dynasts:** At roster creation, choose up to 3 melee weapons to be heirloom weapons (max 1 per fighter). When attacking with an heirloom weapon, add **+1** to the roll to hit. If the bearer is Slain, the weapon passes to the highest-Mt friendly fighter. Ransom cost +10 Crowns if captured with the weapon.

**Revelers:** At the start of a friendly fighter's activation, they may suffer **1 Wound** to gain **+2" M** until end of that activation.

**Courtiers:** This retinue starts with **+100 Crowns**. Each fighter starts each battle with 1 fewer Wound than their W value (minimum 1).

#### Mortal

**Zealots:** When a friendly fighter attacks an enemy **Caster**, add **+1** to the roll to hit.

**Constables:** Enemy fighters cannot **Retreat** while within engagement range of a friendly fighter from this retinue.

**Alchemists:** This retinue may buy alchemical weapons and consumables without rolling on the Shadow Market table.

**Ironbound:** Friendly fighters within **1"** of at least one other friendly fighter gain **Fear**.

## Step 2 — Set Your Crown Budget

Start with **1000 Crowns**. Track every purchase as you build. Your total roster cost must not exceed this budget.

Traditions that modify costs (Spellblades, Beastmen, Vampires, etc.) apply when you recruit fighters or buy gear in later steps.

## Step 3 — Recruit Fighters

### Fighter count


| Archetype | Minimum | Target | Maximum |
| --------- | ------- | ------ | ------- |
| Knights   | 5       | 5–8    | 10      |
| Hunters   | 5       | 6–10   | 12      |
| Folk      | 6       | 8–12   | 15      |
| Cult      | 5       | 5–8    | 10      |


Every retinue must include exactly **1 Leader**. Fill remaining slots using the class names and caps below.

### Class names by Archetype


| Generic class  | Knights      | Hunters       | Folk             | Cult          |
| -------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| **Leader**     | Lord (1)     | Captain (1)   | Mayor (1)        | Theurge (1)   |
| **Elite**      | Knight (0–4) | Stalker (0–4) | Guildsman (0–3)  | —             |
| **Specialist** | Squire (0–5) | Tracker (0–3) | Militiaman (0–5) | Adept (0–2)   |
| **Rank**       | —            | Hand (0–4)    | Townsfolk (0–6)  | Acolyte (0–7) |


### Class profiles

Apply one profile per fighter. No stat may exceed **5** at creation, except species baselines that already start at 4 (Elf Sk/Wi, Dwarf Mt). Use the class names and slot caps from the table above.


| Generic class  | Profile                                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Leader**     | Species baseline + **+1** to one stat (CC, RC, Mt, Sk, Wi, or Sa). May take **Caster** **or** the class +1 stat bonus — not both. |
| **Elite**      | **+1 Mt** or **+1 Sk**.                                                                                                           |
| **Specialist** | **+1 CC** or **+1 RC**.                                                                                                           |
| **Rank**       | Species baseline; no class bonus.                                                                                                 |


**Knights** have no Rank class. **Cult** has no Elite class — use these profiles instead:

- **Theurge** (Leader): **Caster** (required) + **+1** to one stat.
- **Adept** (Specialist): **Caster** (required). Wi 4+, 3 Domain spells. Does not take the Specialist stat bonus.
- **Acolyte** (Rank): does not start with **Caster**.

### Caster

**Mortal Domain:** no fighter may take **Caster**.

If your Domain is not **Mortal**, your retinue's **Leader** **may** take **Caster** at creation — optional for **Knights**, **Hunters**, and **Folk**; required for **Cult** (**Theurge**). **Caster** replaces the class stat bonus; there is no extra Crown cost.

**Cult** is the only Archetype that may give **Caster** to a non-Leader at creation. Field up to **2 Adepts**; each **Adept** **must** take **Caster**. **Acolytes** do not start with **Caster**.


| Archetype | Max fighters with Caster |
| --------- | ------------------------ |
| Knights   | 1                        |
| Hunters   | 1                        |
| Folk      | 1                        |
| Cult      | 3                        |


A fighter with **Caster**:

- Has **Wi 4+** (raise Wi to 4 at recruitment if needed)
- Knows **3 spells** from the retinue's Domain list (see [Magic](magic.md))
- May take the **Cast** action in battle

Choose spells freely from your Domain list. Each Domain's standard attack spell (Radiant Strike, Arcane Bolt, etc.) counts as one of your 3 choices.

### Feat picks — Proficiency

When you recruit each fighter, assign feats. Most picks are **Proficiency** —
the first feat on your Archetype list. **Mortal** retinues also have
**Proficiency — Firearms** on the Domain feat list.


| Class          | Feat picks                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Leader**     | 2 — usually **Proficiency** from your Archetype and/or Mortal lists                         |
| **Elite**      | 2 — usually **Proficiency** from your Archetype and/or Mortal lists                         |
| **Specialist** | 1 chosen feat + 1 built-in proficiency (see [Feats](feats.md#built-in-proficiencies-specialist)) |
| **Rank**       | 1 — usually **Proficiency** from your Archetype list                                        |


A **Rank** fighter with only **Archery** equips bows and crossbows but not
melee weapons. A **Rank** fighter with only **One-Handed** equips sword and
shield but not bows.

Full rules: [Feats — Archetype Feats](feats.md#archetype-feats) · [Gear — Weapon proficiencies](gear.md#weapon-proficiencies).

### Fighter costs (Human baseline)


| Class                                           | Cost |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| Leader (Lord, Captain, Mayor, Theurge)          | 125  |
| Elite (Knight, Stalker, Guildsman)              | 75   |
| Specialist (Squire, Tracker, Militiaman, Adept) | 60   |
| Rank (Hand, Townsfolk, Acolyte)                 | 40   |



| Modifier     | Cost |
| ------------ | ---- |
| Elf or Dwarf | +10  |


Apply any Tradition cost modifiers (e.g. Spellblades +5 per fighter) when calculating each fighter's cost.

## Step 4 — Choose Species

Every fighter uses a species baseline. Apply the class profile from Step 3 on top of that baseline.


| Species   | Premium    | Profile                                       |
| --------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Human** | —          | M 6", CC 3, RC 3, Mt 3, Sk 3, Wi 3, Sa 4, W 3 |
| **Elf**   | +10 Crowns | M 7", CC 3, RC 3, Mt 3, Sk 4, Wi 4, Sa 3, W 3 |
| **Dwarf** | +10 Crowns | M 5", CC 3, RC 3, Mt 4, Sk 3, Wi 3, Sa 3, W 3 |


Species premium applies once per fighter, regardless of class.

---

## Step 5 — Equip Your Fighters

Equip each fighter only with weapons they are **proficient** in (from Step 3
**Proficiency**). Buy gear from [Gear](gear.md) within your Archetype's armor,
shield, and firearm tier limits.

Each fighter has **3 weapon slots** (see [Gear — Weapon slots](gear.md#weapon-slots)). Plan loadouts so no fighter exceeds 3 slots total.

---

## Step 6 — Verify Your Roster

Before your first battle, confirm:

- Archetype + Domain chosen; Cult is not Mortal
- Tradition chosen and its rule recorded
- Fighter count within Archetype min/max
- Exactly 1 Leader (Lord, Captain, Mayor, or Theurge)
- Each class within Archetype slot caps
- Casters: within Archetype max; **Mortal** has none; each has Wi 4+ and 3 Domain spells
- Total cost ≤ Crown budget
- **Retinue Rating** = Roster Cost + sum of Veteran Value on each fighter *(campaign)*
- Each fighter has correct feat picks / built-in proficiencies
- Each equipped weapon matches a fighter's proficiency
- Each fighter within Archetype armor, shield, and firearm tier access
- Each fighter ≤ 3 weapon slots
- No stat above 5 at creation (except species baselines that start at 4)
- Poisons: one per weapon, applied at setup
- Tradition cost modifiers applied (Spellblades, Beastmen, Vampires, etc.)

## Example — Silver Hunt (Hunters + Mortal, 1000 Crowns)

**Tradition:** Alchemists · **Domain:** Mortal · **Archetype:** Hunters

Six fighters, fully equipped:

| Fighter | Class | Proficiencies | Equipment | Fighter | Gear | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Captain | Leader | Firearms, One-Handed | Musket, Dagger | 125 | 110 | 235 |
| Stalker | Elite | Archery, One-Handed | Longbow, Hand Axe | 75 | 70 | 145 |
| Stalker | Elite | Archery, Thrown | Crossbow, Silversbane *(on Crossbow)* | 75 | 130 | 205 |
| Tracker | Specialist | Firearms *(built-in)*, One-Handed | Pistol, Dagger, Smoke Bomb | 60 | 125 | 185 |
| Hand | Rank | Archery | Shortbow | 40 | 40 | 80 |
| Hand | Rank | One-Handed | Hand Axe | 40 | 20 | 60 |
| **Total** | | | | **415** | **475** | **890** |

Every fighter stays within **3 weapon slots**, proficiency limits, and Hunters
equipment access. **110 Crowns** remain unspent.
