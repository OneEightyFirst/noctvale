# Creating a Retinue

Your **retinue** is the warband you field in skirmish battles and campaigns — a roster of fighters, their equipment, and (if your Domain allows) their spells. This guide walks you through building a legal roster from scratch.

Every retinue is built in two layers:

1. **Identity** — Archetype, Domain, and Tradition. This sets how your retinue fights and what powers it can wield.
2. **Roster** — spend **Crowns** to recruit fighters, buy gear, and apply poisons.

---

## Your Budget

| Game type | Starting Crowns | Retinue Rating |
|---|---|---|
| Skirmish / new campaign | **1000** | **1000** (Veteran Value 0 on every fighter) |

- Every Crown cost in this guide is a multiple of **5 Crowns**.
- At a typical roster size (7–10 fighters), plan to spend roughly **450–550 Crowns** on fighters and **450–550 Crowns** on weapons, armor, and consumables.
- **Named factions** (Phoenix Guard, Silver Hunt, Village Watch, etc.) are optional flavor presets — the same rules apply whether you use one or build your own combination.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Your Archetype

Your **Archetype** is the foundation of your retinue — roster size, fighter classes, equipment access, and how many casters you may field. Pick one, then open its chapter and build from there.

| Archetype | Identity | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Knights** | Elite armored companies, 5–10 fighters | **[Knights](archetypes/knights.md)** |
| **Hunters** | Versatile skirmishers and slayers, 5–12 fighters | **[Hunters](archetypes/hunters.md)** |
| **Folk** | Numerous townsfolk and militia, 6–15 fighters | **[Folk](archetypes/folk.md)** |
| **Cult** | Magic-dominant brotherhoods, 5–10 fighters | **[Cult](archetypes/cult.md)** |

Each archetype chapter includes:

- Fluff and table identity
- Domain and Tradition options for that Archetype
- Species table, fighter-type profiles, Crown costs, and feat lists
- Caster rules and equipment limits
- Example factions and (where available) example rosters
- An archetype-specific roster checklist

Work through your archetype chapter before returning here for shared steps.

---

## Step 2 — Species and Recruitment Cost

Each archetype chapter has a **Species** table (M through W, **Cost** column).
Pick a species for each fighter, then add the species **Cost** to that fighter
type's recruitment cost (e.g. Elf Theurge: 125 + 10 = **135 Crowns**). Apply
fighter-type stat bonuses from the same chapter on top of the species row.

Full species rules: [Core Rules](core-rules.md)

---

## Step 3 — Equip Your Fighters

Equip each fighter only with weapons they are **proficient** in (assigned during roster creation in your archetype chapter). Buy gear from [Gear](gear.md) within your Archetype's armor, shield, and firearm tier limits.

Each fighter has **3 weapon slots** (see [Gear — Weapon slots](gear.md#weapon-slots)). Plan loadouts so no fighter exceeds 3 slots total.

- Apply **Flintlock** to any firearm if desired (−25 Crowns, Single Shot — see [Gear](gear.md))
- Purchase **alchemy** and apply poisons during setup (one poison per weapon — see [Gear — Equipment](gear.md#equipment))

---

## Step 4 — Verify Your Roster

Before your first battle, confirm the checklist at the end of your archetype chapter, plus these shared items:

- Total cost ≤ Crown budget
- **Retinue Rating** = Roster Cost + sum of Veteran Value on each fighter *(campaign)*
- Poisons: one per weapon, applied at setup
- Tradition cost modifiers applied (Spellblades, Beastmen, Vampires, etc.)

---

## Campaign Retinues

After your first roster is built, you maintain and expand it between battles using earnings from sold Relics. The same building rules apply — see [Economy](../campaign/economy.md) and [Post-Game](../campaign/post-game.md).
