# Noctvale Playtest Rules

These rules are additive. A fighter, weapon, feat, spell, item, or retinue may
use only the rules and access printed for it.

## Play Materials

Each player needs miniatures or tokens on bases, a **3' x 3'** battlefield with
terrain, at least **10 d6** in two colors, a measuring tool marked in **"**,
counters for **Hidden**, **Downed**, **Stunned**, **Out of Action**, and
**Overwatch**, and template markers.

Use scenario rules when a battle gives different setup, terrain, deployment,
objective, or reward instructions.

## Stats and Checks

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| M | Movement (") |
| CC | Close Combat |
| RC | Ranged Combat |
| Mt | Might |
| Sk | Skill |
| Wi | Will |
| Sa | Sanity |
| W | Wounds |

A stat check succeeds on **1d6 + stat >= 8**. A natural **1** always fails. A
natural **6** always succeeds. In campaign play, no stat can be raised above
**6**. If a stat is reduced to **1**, the fighter can no longer perform actions
that require that stat.

A die can be rerolled only once. If more than one rule allows a reroll, choose
one; the second result stands.

## Retinue Building

Start a skirmish or new campaign retinue with **1000 Crowns**. Record
**Retinue Rating** as **Roster Cost** after building.

Every Crown cost is a multiple of **5 Crowns**.

Build in this order:

1. Choose **Archetype**.
2. Choose one **Tradition** allowed for that Archetype; the Tradition sets the
   retinue's **Domain**.
3. Recruit fighters within Archetype count and class caps.
4. Choose each fighter's species and apply class stat bonuses.
5. Choose each fighter's starting feat.
6. Equip fighters.
7. Assign spells to fighters with **Caster**.

Every retinue includes exactly **1 Leader**.

| Archetype | Field | Class caps | Armor access | Firearm access if Mortal | Caster cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Knights** | 5-10 | 1 Lord, 0-4 Knight, 0-5 Squire | Light, Medium, Heavy | Basic | 1 |
| **Hunters** | 5-12 | 1 Captain, 0-4 Stalker, 0-3 Tracker, 0-4 Hand | Light, Medium | Basic, Refined | 1 |
| **Folk** | 6-15 | 1 Mayor, 0-3 Guildsman, 0-5 Militiaman, 0-6 Townsfolk | Light | Basic | 1 |
| **Cult** | 5-10 | 1 Theurge, 0-2 Adept, 0-7 Acolyte | By **Magic Armor** feat | - | 3 |

**Basic Firearms:** Musket, Blunderbuss. **Refined Firearms:** Pistol, Long
Rifle. **Mortal** adds **Firearms** to the Proficiency menu and access to
gunpowder weapons. Magic and gunpowder do not mix in the same retinue. **Cult**
cannot choose **Mortal**.

### Keywords

**Caster:** The fighter may take the **Cast** action. Spell choice is free at
list creation. Each Domain's standard attack spell counts toward that fighter's
spell allowance. **Mortal** retinues cannot give **Caster** at recruitment.

**Vampire:** Counts as **Undead** for rules that check **Undead**. The
**Vampires** Tradition gives **Vampire** to each Leader, Elite, and Specialist
at roster creation. Fighters with **Vampire** pay **+2 XP** for each post-game
advancement.

### Feats at Recruitment

When a fighter is recruited, choose **1 Archetype feat** for that fighter. A
Specialist's built-in proficiency does not count as this feat.

Each **Proficiency** pick must choose a different weapon proficiency. A fighter
can equip only weapons they are proficient in, except any fighter may equip a
**Dagger**.

Campaign advancement may grant additional feats later.

### Light Traditions

**Crusaders:** Friendly fighters within **1"** of another friendly fighter add
**+1 Sa** to **Fear**, **Panic**, and **Insanity** tests.

**Paladins:** Once per round, when a friendly fighter becomes **Downed**, one
friendly fighter within **6"** may move up to **3"** toward them. This move
cannot enter engagement range.

**Penitents:** While a friendly fighter has fewer than their starting Wounds,
they gain **+1" M**.

**Luminaries:** At the start of each round, choose one enemy fighter within line
of sight of any friendly fighter. That enemy loses **Hidden**.

### Arcane Traditions

**Spellblades:** Fighters cost **+5 Crowns**. Melee weapons they carry gain
**Arcane**. When attacking with an **Arcane** weapon, change 1 failed Strike
Pool die into a normal hit. The hit keeps its color and cannot become a
critical hit.

**Ritualists:** Before deployment, place one ritual circle wholly within your
deployment zone. Friendly **Caster** fighters within **1"** of it add **+1** to
casting rolls. If they roll a Mishap, they suffer **1 Wound** after resolving
it.

**Sorcerers:** Friendly **Caster** fighters may each know 1 additional spell for
**+10 Crowns**. When a **Caster** who knows an additional spell this way rolls a
Mishap, they suffer **1 Wound** after resolving it.

**Runecasters:** This retinue may buy Rune-stones for **15 Crowns**. Rune-stones
take **1 weapon slot**. Once per battle, after the bearer makes a stat roll,
rolls to hit, rolls defense dice, or makes a casting roll, discard the
Rune-stones to reroll **one die** from that roll. Accept the second result.
Remove the Rune-stones from the roster after the battle.

### Infernal Traditions

**Diabolists:** Once per battle, at the start of a friendly fighter's
activation, that fighter may suffer **1 Wound** to gain **+1 Mt** and **+1 Sa**
until the end of the battle. If that fighter becomes **Downed**, they lose this
bonus and suffer **-1 Mt** and **-1 Sa** until the end of the battle. Sa cannot
be reduced below **1**.

**Binders:** This retinue may recruit Daemons as Rank fighters. Daemon profiles
and recruitment costs are an open roster decision.

**Hellknights:** Armor bought by this retinue costs **+10 Crowns**. Friendly
fighters wearing armor **project Fear**.

**Damned:** Non-**Caster** fighters cost **-10 Crowns**. When rolling on the
Casualty Table for one of those fighters, subtract **1** from the roll.

### Nature Traditions

**Witches:** Once per round, when an enemy fighter within **12"** of a friendly
**Caster** makes a stat roll or casting roll, subtract **1** from that roll.

**Grove-keepers:** After terrain is set, choose one terrain piece outside both
deployment zones. Friendly fighters within **1"** of it add **+1** to
**Recover** rolls.

**Beastmen:** Choose **Wolf**, **Rat**, **Bear**, or **Serpent**. All fighters
cost **+10 Crowns** and gain the chosen rule.

| Beast-mark | Rule |
|---|---|
| Wolf | Fighters gain **Fighting Claws**, a 0-slot melee weapon: **+2 Mt**, **+1 Sk**, Axe. |
| Rat | Fighters may carry 1 additional one-handed weapon. This extra weapon cannot be a shield, firearm, bomb, or two-handed weapon. |
| Bear | Fighters gain **+1 Mt** and **-1 Sa**. Sa cannot be reduced below **1**. |
| Serpent | When a fighter attacks with a melee weapon, 1 unblocked hit may inflict **Poisoned** instead of 1 Wound. |

**Hedge-walkers:** During deployment, up to 2 friendly fighters may start
**Hidden** if each is within **1"** of terrain and outside the enemy deployment
zone.

### Necromancy Traditions

**Bell-keepers:** Once per battle, when a friendly fighter is taken **Out of
Action**, replace that fighter with a **Zombie** until the end of the battle.
Place the Zombie where the fighter was. The Zombie joins your retinue for the
rest of the battle. After the battle, roll on the Casualty Table for the
original fighter. If the original fighter is **Slain** and the Zombie is not
**Out of Action**, the Zombie joins your retinue. Otherwise, remove the Zombie
from your roster.

**Bone-priests:** This retinue may recruit **Skeletons** as Rank fighters up to
its normal Rank cap: up to 4 for Hunters or up to 7 for Cult. Skeletons count
toward maximum fighters and cannot gain XP, buy equipment, carry scenario
rewards, or use post-battle advancement.

**Sepulchers:** While a friendly fighter is **Downed** or **Stunned**, that
fighter **projects Fear**.

**Wightlords:** All fighters cost **+20 Crowns** and gain **Undead** and
**Fearless**.

### Blood Traditions

**Vampires:** At roster creation, each Leader, Elite, and Specialist gains
**Vampire** and costs **+20 Crowns**. Each fighter with **Vampire** chooses **1**
vampire ability; the ability list is TBD. When a fighter with **Vampire** takes
an enemy fighter **Out of Action** with **Melee** or **Mercy Kill**, restore
**1 Wound** to that fighter. Fighters with **Vampire** pay **+2 XP** for each
post-game advancement.

**Dynasts:** At roster creation, choose up to 3 melee weapons as heirloom
weapons. No fighter may carry more than 1 heirloom weapon. When a fighter
attacks with an heirloom weapon, add **+1** to the roll to hit. If the bearer is
**Slain**, the weapon passes to the friendly fighter with the highest Mt; choose
among tied fighters. If the bearer is captured, the opposing retinue keeps the
heirloom weapon but cannot use it. If the original retinue ransoms the captured
fighter, add **+10 Crowns** to the ransom to recover the weapon.

**Revelers:** At the start of a friendly fighter's activation, they may suffer
**1 Wound** to gain **+2" M** until the end of that activation.

**Courtiers:** This retinue starts with **+100 Crowns**. Each fighter starts
each battle with 1 fewer Wound than their W value, to a minimum of **1 Wound**.

### Mortal Traditions

**Zealots:** When a friendly fighter attacks an enemy **Caster**, add **+1** to
the roll to hit.

**Constables:** Enemy fighters cannot **Retreat** while within engagement range
of a friendly fighter from this retinue.

**Alchemists:** This retinue may buy alchemical weapons and alchemical
consumables without rolling on the Shadow Market table.

**Ironbound:** Friendly fighters within **1"** of another friendly fighter
**project Fear**.

## Feats

A fighter may gain feats from matching **Archetype**, **Domain**, and
**Universal** lists. If two copies of the same named feat affect one fighter at
the same time, apply it once.

The Feat Advancement Table is not written. Until it is available, use the
Keyword Advancement Table when a Feat roll is called for.

### Firearms and Bombs

Firearms use **Ranged**, require **Firearms** proficiency and **Mortal**, use a
flat Strike Pool, and gain critical hits against all targets. Bombs also require
**Firearms** proficiency and **Mortal**.

**Primer Roll (2d6 + Sk):** Roll against the firearm's primer difficulty.
Profile values assume **Sk 3**. At other Sk, use **primer difficulty - 3 + Sk**.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Double 1s | **Misfire** |
| Below difficulty | Gun fails to fire; action spent |
| Meets or exceeds difficulty | Gun fires; resolve Attack Sequence |
| Double 6s | **Overcharge**: all Strike Pool dice auto-succeed as hits; still roll for natural 6 criticals |

**Brace of Pistols:** Holster equipment, not a weapon. Holds 2 purchased
**Pistols** in 1 weapon slot. Requires **Firearms** and **Mortal**. Without a
Brace, each Pistol uses 1 weapon slot.

**Fire a Pistol:** Use one **Ranged** action, choose one equipped **Pistol**, and
resolve its Primer Roll and Attack Sequence.

**Bombs:** Use **Ranged**. Declare a target point or direction, then make the
Primer Roll.

| Bomb result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Double 1s | Bomb explodes in the thrower's hand; center blast on thrower |
| Fail | Bomb scatters; roll **d6 + Mt** and move detonation point that many inches from the declared target by scatter die |
| Pass | Bomb is on target; roll **d6 + Mt** for maximum distance and place blast along the line to the declared target |

All fighters under the blast suffer the Strike Pool.

### Armor and Shields

Armor and shields convert failed defense dice into successes after rolling the
defense pool.

| Armor | Conversion |
|---|---|
| Light Armor | 2 failed red or blue defense dice -> 1 normal success; if spending 1 red and 1 blue, choose the success color |
| Medium Armor | 1 failed red defense die -> 1 normal success |
| Heavy Armor | 1 failed red defense die -> 1 normal success, or 2 failed red defense dice -> 1 critical success |

Heavy Armor's critical success is the only equipment effect that can block
incoming critical hits.

| Shield | Conversion |
|---|---|
| Buckler | 1 failed blue defense die -> 1 normal success |
| Shield | 1 failed red defense die + 1 failed blue defense die -> normal successes |
| Tower Shield | 2 failed red defense dice + 1 failed blue defense die -> normal successes |

Armor and shield conversions stack. The defender chooses which failed defense
dice to convert.

### Sphere of Influence

A **Sphere of Influence** affects valid targets within range, measured base to
base. The bearer is affected by their own Sphere. Spheres do not require line of
sight. The source must be **Active**; Spheres are suppressed while the source is
**Downed**, **Stunned**, or **Out of Action**. Spheres from the same item or
Companion type do not stack.

## Battle Setup and Rounds

### Standard Battle Setup

1. Determine initiative. The player with fewer activations has initiative. If
   tied, roll **2d6**; high result has initiative; reroll ties.
2. Initiative player chooses a board edge. Opponent uses the opposite edge.
   Deployment zones extend **6"** inward.
3. Each player divides their retinue into **3 deployment groups** as evenly as
   possible.
4. Starting with initiative, players alternate deploying one group at a time.

### Round Sequence

At the start of each round, each player's **activation count** is their Active
and Downed fighters on the board. Do not count **Stunned**, **Out of Action**,
or **Escaped** fighters. Do not recalculate activation count during the round.

First round uses setup initiative. Later rounds, the lower activation count has
initiative. If tied, the player who had initiative last round keeps it. The
player with the lower activation count gains **Overwatch tokens** equal to the
difference.

Starting with initiative, players alternate activating one fighter at a time.
Each activated fighter has **2 actions** in any order and combination.
**Stunned**, **Out of Action**, and **Escaped** fighters cannot be chosen. If
one player runs out of activations first, the other activates remaining fighters
normally; the out-of-fighters player may spend unspent Overwatch tokens as
reactions.

The round ends when every fighter who can activate has activated or skipped
activation.

## Actions

| Action | Cost | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| **Move** | 1 | Move up to **M**. May move through friendly fighters. Cannot end on another fighter's base. |
| **Charge** | 1 | Move up to **M** and end within **1"** of an enemy. May move through friendly fighters. Cannot end on another fighter's base. |
| **Climb** | 1 | Move vertically at half **M**. Cannot end within engagement range. |
| **Scramble** | 1 | Move at half **M** through difficult terrain. Cannot end within engagement range. |
| **Jump** | 1 | Move across open space up to **M**. Roll **d6 + Sk >= 8**. Cannot end within engagement range. |
| **Retreat** | 1 | Leave engagement range. Roll **d6 + Sk >= 8**. On failure, opponent may make a free **Melee** attack with the full Attack Sequence if not engaged with another enemy. |
| **Melee** | 1 | Attack an enemy within engagement range with a melee weapon. Resolve Attack Sequence. |
| **Ranged** | 1 | Attack with a ranged weapon. Target must be in range and line of sight. Bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, and bombs use their rules. Firearms roll Primer Roll before Attack Sequence. |
| **Cast** | 1 | Cast a spell. May be used while in melee. Enemy targets need range and line of sight unless the spell says otherwise. Does not trigger Ranged Reaction. |
| **Aim** | 1 | The fighter's next **Ranged** action this activation gains **+1 die** of the weapon's dominant color. Natural 6s are critical hits on that Ranged action. Dominant color is the higher +Mt or +Sk; if tied, choose. |
| **Mercy Kill** | 1 | A **Stunned** enemy within **1"** is immediately **Out of Action**. |
| **Brace** | 1 | Gain **+1 red defense die** until this fighter's next activation. |
| **Hide** | 1 | Become **Hidden** while within **1"** of terrain. |
| **Overwatch** | Skip rest of activation | Give this fighter an Overwatch token. |
| **Interact** | 1 | Open doors, operate scenario objects, or pick up items. |
| **Loot** | 1 | Search a building for valuables in scenarios that use Looting Buildings. |
| **Recover** | 1 | A **Downed** fighter rolls on the Recover table. |
| **Help** | 1 | Assist a **Downed** or **Stunned** friendly fighter within **1"**. |

**Engagement range** is **1"**. There is no facing system; fighters are aware in
all directions.

**Hidden:** A Hidden fighter cannot be targeted by **Ranged** or **Cast** beyond
**6"**. They lose Hidden when performing **Melee**, **Ranged**, **Cast**,
**Aim**, **Mercy Kill**, **Charge**, **Climb**, **Jump**, or when moving within
**6"** of an enemy. They may **Move**, **Scramble**, **Brace**, **Interact**,
**Loot**, **Recover**, or **Help** without losing Hidden if they remain within
**1"** of terrain.

**Overwatch reaction actions:** **Move**, **Ranged**, **Charge**, **Melee**, or
**Retreat** after an enemy fighter completes an action.

**Escape:** During a **Move** action, a fighter may leave through a board edge
if they have enough movement. Remove them. They have **Escaped**, cannot return,
are not **Out of Action**, and do not roll on the Casualty Table.

**Ending the battle:** The battle ends immediately when only one retinue has
fighters remaining on the board, unless the scenario says otherwise. That
retinue wins.

## Conditions

Wound states move in this order:

`Active -> Downed -> Stunned -> Out of Action`

### Downed

A fighter becomes **Downed** at **0 Wounds**. A Downed fighter cannot attack,
cannot take normal actions, may use **Recover**, and retains their full defense
pool. Any unblocked hit on a Downed fighter pushes them to **Stunned**.

| Recover 1d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Become **Stunned** |
| 2-3 | Remain **Downed** |
| 4-5 | Stand with **1 Wound** |
| 6 | Stand with **1 Wound** and perform one additional action |

### Stunned

A fighter becomes **Stunned** from a Recover roll of **1** or an unblocked hit
while Downed. A Stunned fighter cannot move, act, Recover, or be chosen for
activation; does not count toward activation count; and defends with **red dice
only**. Any unblocked hit on a Stunned fighter pushes them **Out of Action**.
**Mercy Kill** bypasses defense.

If a Stunned fighter becomes Active during a round before their player has
finished activating fighters, they may activate later this round with **1
action**. If they become Downed, they may activate later this round but can only
use **Recover**. Do not recalculate initiative or Overwatch tokens.

### Help

| Help 1d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fighter goes **Out of Action** |
| 2-3 | No effect |
| 4-5 | Fighter becomes **Downed** if Stunned |
| 6 | Fighter stands with **1 Wound** |

### Afflictions

Afflictions last for the battle unless a rule sets another duration.

| Affliction | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Poisoned** | **-1 Mt**. |
| **Weakened** | **-1 Mt**, **-1 Sk**, and **-1" M** until the effect ends. |
| **Enfeebled** | **-1 Mt** and **-1 Sk** until the effect ends. |
| **Bleeding** | Suffer **1 Wound** immediately. At the start of each activation, roll **d6 + Wi >= 8**. Pass: remove Bleeding. Fail: suffer **1 Wound**. |

## Combat

Red dice use **Mt**. Blue dice use **Sk**. Weapons add red dice with **+Mt** and
blue dice with **+Sk**.

### Attack Sequence

1. **Build the Strike Pool:** Fighter **Mt + Sk** plus weapon **+Mt / +Sk**.
   Firearms and damage spells use a flat Strike Pool.
2. **Roll to hit:** Roll each Strike Pool die. **Melee:** **d6 + CC >= 8**.
   **Ranged:** **d6 + RC >= 8**. Natural **1** misses. Natural **6** hits.
3. **Determine criticals:** Natural 6s are critical hits when the attacker has
   weapon triangle advantage, Hammer vs Heavy Armor, magic triangle advantage,
   **Aim**, or a firearm. Critical hits cannot be blocked except by Heavy
   Armor critical success. Critical hits retain their color.
4. **Roll defense:** Base **1 red + 1 blue**, plus comparison dice. Roll red
   defense as **d6 + Mt >= 8** and blue defense as **d6 + Sk >= 8**. Armor and
   shields convert failed defense dice into successes.
5. **Apply Wounds:** Each unblocked hit inflicts **1 Wound**. At 0 Wounds, a
   fighter becomes **Downed**. An unblocked hit on Downed pushes to **Stunned**.
   An unblocked hit on Stunned pushes to **Out of Action**.

Unarmed attacks use base Mt and Sk.

### Defense Pool

Base defense is **1 red die + 1 blue die**.

Add **+1 red defense die** for each point defender's Mt exceeds attacker's Mt.
Add **+1 blue defense die** for each point defender's Sk exceeds attacker's Sk.
Attacker excess gives no penalty.

Successful defense dice block hits:

- Red defense blocks red hits.
- Blue defense blocks blue hits.
- Two red successes may block one blue hit.
- Two blue successes may block one red hit.
- Normal successes cannot block critical hits.
- Heavy Armor critical success can block critical hits.

### Criticals

Weapon triangle:

`Sword > Axe > Spear > Sword`

Only **Sword**, **Axe**, and **Spear** participate. **Hammer** is outside the
triangle. Hammer attacks score critical hits on natural 6s only against targets
wearing **Heavy Armor**. If either fighter uses a Hammer, weapon triangle
advantage does not apply.

Magic triangles:

- **Mystic:** `Light > Infernal > Arcane > Light`
- **Natural:** `Nature > Necromancy > Blood > Nature`

Firearms score critical hits against all targets.

### Ranged Reaction

When a fighter is targeted by a **Ranged** action, after the attack fully
resolves, the target may perform **one free action** from the action list. The
reaction triggers when selected as a target, whether the attack hits or misses.
If the target is **Downed** or **Out of Action** after the attack, they cannot
react. A fighter cannot perform a Ranged Reaction during their own player's
turn. **Cast** does not trigger Ranged Reaction. Multiple Ranged attacks in one
activation trigger one reaction per attack. A Ranged Reaction cannot trigger
another Ranged Reaction.

### Table Rules

**Line of sight:** Use the firing fighter's viewpoint. Use the target's body on
and above its base. Ignore weapons, banners, cloaks, and parts extending past
the base. Friendly fighters do not block line of sight. Enemy fighters do.

**Friendly fighters:** A fighter may move through friendly fighters during
**Move**, **Charge**, **Scramble**, **Climb**, and **Jump**. They cannot end
movement on another fighter's base.

**Cover:** If intervening terrain lies between attacker and target and is more
than **1"** from the attacker, the target has cover. Cover grants **+1 blue
defense die** against **Ranged** attacks and spells using the Attack Sequence.

**Friendly in the line of fire:** When a firearm attack or damage spell misses a
declared enemy and a friendly fighter's base lies between attacker and target,
roll **1d6**. On **1**, the closest intervening friendly suffers 1 hit from that
attack. Defense applies. This applies only if the primer or casting roll
succeeded and the declared target took no wounds. It does not apply to Mishap,
Misfire, failed casting, failed firearm primer, bows, crossbows, or throwing
weapons.

**Clustered enemies:** When declaring **Ranged** or **Cast** against a target
with other enemy fighters from the same retinue within **1"**, roll **d6 + Sk >=
8**. Pass: target intended fighter. Fail with one other enemy: that fighter is
the new target. Fail with two or more: randomly choose among all enemy fighters
within **1"** of the declared target, including the declared target.

**Range:** Targets closer than minimum or beyond maximum range cannot be
targeted. There are no distance modifiers. Most ranged weapons have minimum
range **3"**. Pistols have no minimum range.

**Falling:** When a fighter falls, roll **d6 + Sk >= 8**. For every **2"**
fallen, roll one die; each **4+** causes **1 Wound**.

**Multiple Engagement:** A fighter may split attacks among standing enemies. If
one enemy is standing and another is Downed or Stunned, the standing enemy must
be prioritized.

**Difficult terrain:** Agree before the battle which area terrain is difficult.
Fighters cannot **Move** or **Charge** through it. Use **Scramble** to cross it
at half **M**.

**Stat modifiers:** Bonuses and penalties to **M**, **CC**, **RC**, **Mt**,
**Sk**, **Wi**, and **Sa** stack. The total modifier to any single stat cannot
exceed **+3** or **-3**. Afflictions count toward this cap.

**Gang Up:** If a friendly fighter is also engaged with the same enemy, add **+1
die** to the attacker's Strike Pool. The attacker chooses red or blue.

## Sanity

Roll **d6 + Sa >= 8** for Sanity tests. Natural **1** fails. Natural **6**
succeeds.

Fighters and effects may **project Fear**, **project Panic**, or **project
Insanity**. When a fighter activates within range and line of sight of such a
source, test Sanity. Default range is **6"** and line of sight unless the rule
prints another range or a melee-only trigger.

On a pass, the fighter is immune to that source for the rest of the game. On a
failure, apply the effect immediately. At the start of that fighter's next
activation, test again before spending actions. Repeat until the fighter passes
or the source is removed.

| Effect | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Fear** | Cannot move closer to the source for the rest of the turn. May otherwise act. |
| **Panic** | Cannot move closer to the source and must spend at least 1 action moving full movement directly away. |
| **Insanity** | Roll **1d6** on the Insanity table. |
| **Fearless** | Immune to Fear, Panic, and Insanity. |

| Insanity 1d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Frenzy:** Attack the closest fighter chosen by the opposing player. It cannot be the Insanity source. |
| 2-3 | **Flee:** Spend both actions moving toward the nearest board edge. |
| 4-5 | **Freeze:** Both actions are spent doing nothing. |
| 6 | **Berserk:** If primary weapon is melee, charge the source and add **2 red dice** and **1 blue die** to Strike Pool, but subtract **1** from roll to hit. If primary weapon is ranged, spend both actions firing at the source and subtract **1** from roll to hit. |

## Scenarios

Choose or roll a scenario before setting up the battlefield. The scenario sets
battlefield location, terrain, deployment, objectives, ending condition, and
rewards. Random reward rolls use the Reward Roll Rule.

### Looting Buildings

Before deployment, players agree which terrain pieces are **buildings**. A
fighter inside a building may use **Loot** to search it. Each building can be
looted once per battle. Mark it searched after resolving the roll. Loot found is
added to post-battle rewards and cannot be equipped or spent during the battle.

**Many Hands:** If 2 or more friendly fighters are inside the same building when
a fighter loots it, add **+1** to the loot roll. A modified result cannot exceed
**11**. Check natural double 1 and double 6 before modifiers.

| 2d6 | Find |
|---|---|
| 2 | **Mishap: Trap**: resolve 2 red hits and 2 blue hits against the fighter; defense applies |
| 3-4 | **Empty Room**: no reward |
| 5-6 | **Loose Goods**: gain **5 Crowns** |
| 7-8 | **Useful Find**: gain **10 Crowns** |
| 9-10 | **Basic Weapon**: after the battle, gain 1 legal Basic weapon |
| 11 | **Relic**: set aside 1 recovered Relic until Economy |
| 12 | **Bonus: Hidden Cache**: set aside 1 recovered Relic and gain **10 Crowns** |

### Loot the Dead

| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| Battlefield Location | Roll on Battlefield Location Table. |
| Terrain | **3' x 3'** board with high-density terrain. |
| Deployment | Use standard deployment. |
| Escape | Fighters may Escape through any board edge with **Move**. |
| End | Battle ends when only one retinue has fighters on board. That retinue wins. |

**Rifling Their Pockets:** When a fighter takes an enemy **Out of Action** with
**Melee** or **Mercy Kill**, roll **2d6**:

| 2d6 | Crowns Found |
|---|---:|
| 2 | **Mishap: Hidden Blade**: searching fighter suffers 1 red hit and 1 blue hit; defense applies |
| 3-5 | 0 |
| 6-8 | 5 |
| 9-10 | 10 |
| 11 | 15 |
| 12 | **Bonus: Heavy Purse**: 20 |

Add Crowns found and Crowns carried by the defeated fighter to the victorious
fighter's purse. A fighter may carry up to **50 Crowns**; excess is lost.
Fighters taken **Out of Action** by ranged attacks, spells, or other effects
cannot be searched.

**Rewards:** Each retinue keeps Crowns carried by **Escaped** fighters. Winner
also keeps Crowns carried by fighters remaining on the board and recovers **2
Relics**.

### The Forest Creeps Ever Onward

| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| Battlefield Location | **Hidden Grove**. |
| Terrain | **3' x 3'** board, normal-density terrain, several buildings. Place movable tree pieces along both side edges not used as deployment edges. |
| Deployment | Use standard deployment. The two board edges not chosen as deployment edges are possible forest edges. |
| Looting | Use **Looting Buildings**. Relics found are recovered by the finding retinue. |

At end of round 1, roll **1d6**: **1-3** left forest edge, **4-6** right forest
edge, judged from the initiative player's deployment edge. The forest advances
from that edge for the battle.

| End of Round | Forest Covers |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 6" |
| 2 | 12" |
| 3 | 18" |
| 4 | 24" |
| 5 | 30" |
| 6 | 36" |

Move tree pieces inward to mark the forest edge. Any fighter inside the covered
area is taken **Out of Action**.

Fighters may Escape through any edge except the advancing forest edge. The
battle ends immediately when all fighters have **Escaped** or been taken **Out
of Action**. If any fighters remain on the board at end of round 6, they are
taken **Out of Action**.

Winner is the retinue that recovered the most Relics through Looting Buildings.
Tie: retinue with more Escaped fighters wins. Still tied: draw. Each retinue
keeps recovered Relics. Winner recovers **2 additional Relics**.

### There's a Reason They Have a Horn

| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| Battlefield Location | **Hidden Grove**. |
| Terrain | **3' x 3'** board, normal-density terrain, central clearing roughly **10"** across, several buildings around the clearing edge. |
| Deployment | Use standard deployment. Place the unicorn at the center of the clearing after both retinues deploy. |
| Looting | Use **Looting Buildings**. |

For targeting, engagement, and attacks, the unicorn and every fighter in both
retinues count as enemies.

| M | CC | RC | Mt | Sk | Wi | Sa | W |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8" | 4 | - | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

**Horn and Hooves:** The unicorn's **Melee** attacks use **+2 Mt / +0 Sk**. If
the unicorn's **Melee** immediately follows **Charge** during the same
activation, add **1 red die** to the Strike Pool.

**Fear (8"):** The unicorn projects **Fear (8")**.

At **0 Wounds**, the unicorn is taken **Out of Action**. Place the Horn token in
base contact with the unicorn before removing it.

At the end of each round, before checking battle end, the unicorn activates if
it is not **Out of Action**. It performs **2 actions**, resolving each in order.
If at least one fighter is within engagement range, it makes **Melee** against
the closest engaged fighter. If none are engaged, it **Charges** the nearest
fighter it can reach. If it cannot Charge, it **Moves** up to **8"** toward the
nearest fighter by the shortest route. Randomly determine ties.

A fighter within **1"** of the Horn token may use **Interact** to pick it up. A
fighter carrying the Horn token may **Escape** through any edge with **Move**.
If the carrier is taken **Out of Action**, place the Horn token in base contact
with that fighter before removing them.

The battle ends immediately when a fighter Escapes with the Horn token; that
fighter's retinue wins. If no fighter Escapes with the Horn token, the battle
ends at end of round 6.

At end of round 6:

- A retinue with a fighter carrying the Horn token wins.
- If the Horn token is on the board, the retinue whose fighter took the unicorn
  **Out of Action** wins.
- If the unicorn is not **Out of Action**, it escapes and the battle is a draw.

Each retinue keeps Relics recovered through Looting Buildings. The winner
recovers **2 Relics** and gains **30 Crowns**. If a fighter Escaped with the
Horn token, that fighter gains **+1 Experience**.

## Campaign

After each battle, resolve the Post-Game Sequence in order:

1. **Scenario Results:** Resolve victory rewards and Relics recovered during the
   battle. Set recovered Relics aside until Economy.
2. **Casualties:** Each fighter who ended **Out of Action** rolls on the
   Casualty Table. Fighters only **Downed** or **Stunned** skip it.
3. **Companion Recovery:** Each Companion who ended **Out of Action** rolls
   **1d6**.
4. **Doom:** Any fighter directed to the Doom Table rolls **2d6**.
5. **Survival Rolls:** Winner rolls on battlefield location table; loser rolls
   Generic. In a draw, both roll Generic.
6. **Economy:** Sell Relics, recruit fighters, and buy gear.
7. **Experience & Advancement:** Award Experience; players may spend it.

### Reward Roll Rule

Random reward rolls use **2d6**. Natural double **1** resolves the Mishap.
Natural double **6** resolves the Bonus. Modifiers change only the total. Check
natural doubles before modifiers. If the natural roll was not double 1 or
double 6, modifiers cannot create result **2** or **12**.

### Experience and Advancement

Experience is a bank per fighter. There are no levels or thresholds. Unspent
Experience carries forward. Spend Experience during post-game to buy
advancement rolls.

| Source | Experience |
|---|---:|
| Fought in battle and was Active at battle end | +1 |
| Went Out of Action but survived Casualty Table | +1 |
| **Hardy** on Casualty Table | +1 |
| **Miraculous** on Doom Table | +2 |
| Retinue achieved a scenario objective | +1 to each fighter, once per fighter |
| Each Relic recovered this battle | +1 to each fighter |
| Took enemy Leader Out of Action | +2 to the fighter who resolved the action |
| Scenario bonus | As written |

A fighter removed as **Slain** gains no Experience from that battle.

| Advancement | XP cost | Roll |
|---|---:|---|
| **Feat** | 2 | 2d6 on Feat Advancement Table; use Keyword Advancement Table until written |
| **Keyword** | 2 | 2d6 on Keyword Advancement Table |
| **Stat** | 5 | 2d6 on Stat Advancement Table |

**Vampire** fighters pay **+2 XP** for each advancement: Feat **4**, Keyword
**4**, Stat **7**.

| Stat Advancement 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | **Setback:** -1 to random stat. Roll d8: 1 M, 2 CC, 3 RC, 4 Mt, 5 Sk, 6 Wi, 7 Sa, 8 W. Minimum 1. |
| 3-4 | **Minor:** +1 to M, CC, or RC; max 6 |
| 5-9 | **Veteran:** +1 to Mt, Sk, Wi, or Sa; max 6 |
| 10-11 | **Elite:** +1 to any stat; max 6 |
| 12 | **Peak:** +1 to any stat and gain **Fearless** if the fighter does not already have it; max 6 |

| Keyword Advancement 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | **Cursed:** Sa -1, minimum 1; no keyword |
| 3-4 | **No change:** take a free Feat roll instead if an eligible feat list is available, or accept nothing |
| 5-6 | **Steady:** +1 Sa permanently, max 6; no new keyword |
| 7 | **Hardened:** gain **Fearless** |
| 8 | **Oathbound:** gain **Fearless** or +1 Wi, max 6 |
| 9-10 | **Gifted:** if eligible, gain **Caster** and assign Domain spells; if not eligible, +1 Wi |
| 11 | **Marked:** gain **Fearless** and +1 CC or +1 RC, max 6 |
| 12 | **Ascendant:** if eligible, gain **Caster**; otherwise +1 to any stat and **Fearless** |

When a fighter gains **Caster** mid-campaign, assign Domain spells by fighter
type: Leader **2**, Adept or Acolyte **1**. Cult Acolytes may gain **Caster**
through advancement. Other Archetypes may gain **Caster** only if the retinue is
under its Caster cap and its Domain is not **Mortal**.

### Retinue Rating

**Retinue Rating** equals **Roster Cost** — the total Crowns spent on fighters
and gear on the roster sheet. Update it whenever you add or remove fighters or
gear.

For campaign matchmaking, pit retinues within **100 Crowns** of rating, or
within **10%** of the higher rating, whichever is larger.
