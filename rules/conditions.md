# Conditions

---

## Wound States

Fighters move through three wound states before being removed from play.

```
Active → Downed → Stunned → Out of Action
```

---

## Downed

A fighter reaches **Downed** when they hit 0 Wounds.

**While Downed, a fighter:**
- Cannot attack
- Cannot perform normal actions
- May attempt the **Recover** action

---

## Recover Action

A Downed fighter may spend an action to attempt recovery.

Roll **1d6:**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Become **Stunned** |
| 2–3 | Remain **Downed** |
| 4–5 | Stand up with **1 Wound** |
| 6 | Stand up with **1 Wound** + perform one additional action |

---

## Stunned

A fighter becomes **Stunned** on a Recover roll of 1.

**While Stunned, a fighter:**
- Cannot move
- Cannot act
- Cannot attempt Recover

If a Stunned fighter is hit by **any attack**, they go **Out of Action**.

---

## Out of Action

A fighter is removed from the game. Post-game consequences apply (injuries, death, etc. — to be developed in campaign rules).

---

## Help Action

A friendly fighter **within 1"** may spend an action to assist a Downed or Stunned ally.

Roll **1d6:**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fighter goes **Out of Action** |
| 2–3 | No effect |
| 4–5 | Fighter becomes **Downed** (if Stunned) |
| 6 | Fighter stands with **1 Wound** |

---

## Mercy Kill

A fighter **within 1"** of a **Stunned** enemy may perform a Mercy Kill.

The Stunned fighter is immediately **Out of Action**.

---

## Design Notes

- Downed creates a dramatic tension window — the fighter isn't dead yet
- The Recover table is risk-based: players have to decide whether to push for a stand-up or accept remaining Downed
- Help has genuine risk (roll of 1 = OOA) which makes it a meaningful decision, not a free action
- Mercy Kill gives aggressive players a reliable way to seal the deal on Stunned enemies
