# Special Rules

---

## Line of Sight

Line of sight is drawn **model to model**.

Parts of the miniature extending beyond the base are **ignored**.

---

## Cover

If **more than half** of the target model is obscured, the target has cover.

**Cover grants +1 Defense Die.**

---

## Falling

Roll Luck when a fighter falls.

For every **2 inches fallen**, roll one die.

Each result of **4+** causes **1 Wound**.

---

## Multiple Engagement

When a fighter is attacking multiple enemies:

- Attacks may be **split** among standing enemies
- If one enemy is standing and another is Downed or Stunned, the **standing enemy must be prioritized**

---

## Overwatch

Overwatch compensates for numerical disadvantage.

**Trigger:** If one warband has fewer fighters than the opposing warband, they receive **Overwatch tokens equal to the difference**.

**Using a token:** A fighter may spend an Overwatch token **instead of activating** to perform a single **reaction action** in response to an enemy action.

### Valid Reaction Actions
- Move
- Shoot
- Charge
- Fight
- Retreat

---

## Gang Up

If a friendly fighter is **also engaged** with the same enemy:

**+1 to the Strike Pool**

---

## Design Notes

- LoS model-to-model (ignoring base overhang) keeps it simple and consistent with most skirmish conventions
- Cover as +1 Df die integrates cleanly with the defense roll system rather than adding a separate save
- Falling damage as a per-2" Luck roll scales naturally with height
- Overwatch as a catch-up mechanic prevents outnumbered players from being steamrolled by activation order
- Gang Up is a small but meaningful reward for coordinated positioning
